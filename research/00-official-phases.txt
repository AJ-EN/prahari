 Two Phases. One Mission. Real Impact.
 The Gujarat Police Innovation Challenge 2026 unfolds in two competitive phases. Phase 1 focuses on developing and validating innovative solutions in a controlled challenge environment. The required CCTV resources and technical documentation are available under the Resources section. The highest-performing teams advance to Phase 2, where their solutions are evaluated in a live operational environment—bringing innovation closer to real-world policing.
 Total Prize Pool 
 ₹ 51,00,000
 STRUCTURE & GUIDELINES
 1. Format
 Phase 1
Sandbox Round
 Stage 01 
 ₹ 18,00,000 Pool 
 Teams will integrate their solutions with the test feeds made available for the challenge across two categories.
 Category 1: Student teams and small & medium startups.
 Category 2: Large startups and established companies.
 The Top 3 teams from each category will receive Phase 1 prizes and advance to the Grand Finale as the six finalist teams.
 Phase 1 prize money will also serve as a grant to support the development and refinement of the final solution.
 Phase 2
Production Round
 Stage 02 
 ₹ 31,00,000 Pool 
 The 6 top-performing teams overall from Phase 1 will advance to the Grand Finale.
 All six finalists will integrate their solutions with real CCTV feeds at scale, irrespective of category.
 Solutions will be evaluated directly by Gujarat Police leadership and a technical jury.
