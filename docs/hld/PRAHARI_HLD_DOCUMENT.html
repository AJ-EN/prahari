COMPONENT DEEP-DIVE & DATA DESIGN

1. Three-Tier Architectural Decomposition

Tier 1: Multi-Agency Camera Ingestion & Synchronization

PRAHARI breaks down legacy departmental data silos across Gujarat by standardizing camera access through three resilient onboarding pathways:

  • Live Catalogue Sync (HTTP/JSON): Polls smart city or sandbox JSON feeds (e.g. cctv.corp8.cloud). Normalizes disparate schemas (flat/nested JSON, GeoJSON coords, varying RTSP/HLS/WHEP endpoints) without hardcoded vendor dependencies. Dropped feeds are gracefully marked absent, never silently deleted.
  • Bulk Operational CSV Import: Ingests large municipal camera lists with automated column alias mapping and coordinate geocoding.
  • Dynamic Single-Camera Registration: Instant REST onboarding for tactical rapid-deployment cameras and mobile police units.

Tier 2: Edge Node Processing & ANPR Pipeline

Designed to operate on standard departmental field laptops (e.g., Apple Silicon M-series or Intel Core i5/i7) without requiring multi-lakh GPU hardware:

  • PyAV Stream Consumer: Employs low-overhead libav bindings over TCP RTSP, utilizing local logging capture and reconnect logic with exponential backoff (1s → 30s ceiling).
  • PTS-Driven Rate Limiter: Presentation Time Stamp (PTS) decimation throttles decoding to 2 FPS, preventing CPU overload while preserving 100% vehicle capture accuracy.
  • Dual-Stage Edge ONNX Model: Compact ~11 MB footprint. Stage 1 localizes the plate boundary box; Stage 2 performs convolutional recurrent neural network (CRNN) sequence recognition in ~33 ms.
  • Spatial Pass Deduplication: Tracks vehicle trajectory across contiguous frames, emitting one unified sighting event at the vehicle's highest-confidence read window.

Tier 3: Central Registry, Intelligence & GIS Operations

Central command microservice built with FastAPI, SQLite WAL mode, and Leaflet.js GIS:

Component Technology Function & Operational Guarantees
Registry Store SQLite (WAL mode) Single-file zero-maintenance database. WAL allows concurrent non-blocking readers during continuous ANPR writes; BEGIN IMMEDIATE guarantees linear transaction isolation.
Fuzzy Matcher Weighted Levenshtein Replaces brittle string matching with an OCR confusion matrix (Z↔2, B↔8, D↔0). Distances ≤ 0.30 raise urgent Hotlist Alerts; distances ≤ 1.10 route to Operator Review.
Route Trace Haversine Kinematics Reconstructs multi-camera vehicle paths chronologically. Computes hop velocity; hops exceeding 140 km/h flag cloned/counterfeit plates.
Alert Bus Server-Sent Events (SSE) Push-model alert bus broadcasting instant notifications and review queues to control-room browser consoles within <50 ms.
Spatial Coverage 1 km² Geo-Grid Engine Vectorized Haversine grid analysis identifying CCTV blindspots, unviable optics, and department coverage distribution.

SECURITY, GOVERNANCE & BENCHMARKS

2. Shield-Lite: Privacy & Tamper-Evident Audit Ledger

CCTV surveillance platforms face severe constitutional scrutiny regarding mass surveillance. PRAHARI implements Shield-Lite to guarantee judicial integrity and statutory compliance:

  • Mandatory Purpose Binding: Endpoints revealing citizen movement (GET /api/trace/{plate}, GET /api/watchlist/search) strictly require a documented purpose and official case_id (e.g. FIR-112/2026). Queries without purpose binding are rejected with HTTP 400.
  • SHA-256 Hash-Chained Audit Ledger: Every query, screening event, and watchlist mutation is written to an append-only SQLite table with an unbreakable SHA-256 hash link:
    hash(i) = SHA-256(hash(i-1) || actor || action || purpose || case_id || params || timestamp)
  • Mathematical Verification: The GET /api/audit/verify API recalculates the entire ledger from genesis. Any retrofitted alteration or record deletion breaks the hash link, ensuring complete courtroom admissibility under the Indian Evidence Act.
